Archive - eviction, cold storage, and the lost gate

Status: shipped (v0.1) - Read when: pending eviction, autoArchive, evictionLead, recall, lost:true, archivePending, from:"parent", expandDigestChars, expand paging or offset, or "a turn disappeared".

What it does

  • Turns a turn that leaves the working window into a pending entry with a mechanical gist and its eroded raw messages.
  • Lets the model archive it with its own gist (optionally keeping the raw), purge it, or leave it.
  • With autoArchive on, archives an unhandled pending turn after evictionLead new user turns, so nothing is irrecoverably lost.
  • Keeps the archive cold: recall refuses without lost:true, and the results are matches, not a replayed window.
  • Lets a delegate search its spawning session's archive with from:"parent" - read-only, so the caller's past is reachable but its record is not writable.
  • Reads a turn back in honest pages, or as an outline, so a turn far larger than the compression limit is reachable instead of silently cut.
  • Caps the archive by turn count and characters, newest wins.

The user-visible behavior is in docs/SPEC.md under "Eviction and the archive".

How & why

Pending is not "everything old"

Only a turn that was in the previous pass's working window and is not in the current one becomes pending. The first pass only seeds seenTurns; a plugin enabled mid-session must not archive turns it never managed. A turn the host already trimmed is skipped: it is not recoverable, and the model had the same chance to fold it.

Age is counted in user turns

age increments only when the window's turn id list changes, which happens on a new user turn, not on a tool continuation. A ten-call turn therefore consumes one eviction lead, not ten. evictionLead is the number of new user turns a pending turn is shown before it is auto-archived (or dropped when autoArchive is off).

Manual archive

archive { turn, gist, keepRaw? } requires a gist and a pending turn id. keepRaw: true keeps the messages for expand; the default is gist only, because the raw is the expensive half. The model's gist replaces the mechanical one.

Auto-archive

With autoArchive on, an unhandled pending turn is archived with its mechanical gist and its raw messages. The raw is kept deliberately: a mechanical gist alone would make expand useless. The model can still purge the entry afterwards.

Recall is gated

Without lost:true, recall returns an instruction, not results. The gate is mechanical because the policy alone is not enough: an archive searched out of habit is a second working set, and the plugin exists to keep the working set small.

recall splits the query into terms (at most eight, all of which must match) and searches the gist and the whole rendered turn. A phrase with gaps still finds its turn, and nothing past an arbitrary cut is invisible. Each match carries up to two short snippets and the character offset each one sits at, which is what turns "maybe this one" into one targeted expand offset:. purge query: stays an exact phrase: a destructive action must not broaden because one term was common.

expand returns the raw turn in pages. mode:"outline" lists every part with its offset, its head and its size, so a 40-part turn fits in one result and names the two pages worth reading. A page ends with (chars 8000–11980 of 171432; continue with offset:11980), or (...; end of turn); a negative offset counts from the end. The page is sized to expandDigestChars clamped by maxResultChars, because a result over the latter is re-eroded - 60% head, 40% tail - on the very next pass. A gist only entry answers with the gist and says so.

A delegate may read the parent's archive

recall and expand accept from:"parent". The field points the read at the session that spawned this one, resolved through the host's parentID (ctx.session.get), so a subagent can search its caller's cold archive and report what it found - the expanded turns enter the child's window, and the parent receives only the child's answer.

The field is read-only by construction: only recall and expand take it; every writing action (patch, ref, archive, purge, notice) refuses it, so a delegate reads its caller's past but can never rewrite the caller's record. The schema offers "parent" as the only value. Four refusals keep it honest: another value, parentArchive:false, a host that cannot answer (no parentOf, or the lookup throws), and a top-level session with no parent.

expand appends the reply budget when the read is delegated: (delegating: your caller keeps at most 4000 chars of your reply; return a digest under that, or write the long version to a file and cite the path). That number is maxResultChars, and it is the real capacity of the channel - a 19,000-character report is cut to 4,000 on the caller's next request - so the contract has to be stated before the child writes, not after.

Caps

trimArchive drops the oldest entries while the archive exceeds archiveMaxTurns or archiveMaxChars. It keeps at least one entry so a single huge turn does not empty the archive.
